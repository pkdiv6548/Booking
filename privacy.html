Privacy & cookies

Prototype privacy notice — update this page with reviewed legal terms before public production use.

Important

This site is a frontend demo. Do not submit real passwords, payment details, identity documents, or other sensitive personal information.

Information handled in this prototype

Search fields and forms run in the browser. Wishlist items and the cookie preference demonstration may be stored in your browser's local storage. There is no booking, payment, or authentication backend connected in this version.

Cookies and local storage

The prototype may store a simple cookie-preference flag and saved property identifiers in local storage so the interface can demonstrate persistent preferences on the same browser.

Payments and reservations

No real payments are accepted and no reservation is sent to a property. Sample prices and listings are illustrative only.

Third-party images

Some demonstration photography is loaded from Unsplash URLs. When connected to the internet, the browser may request those images from the external host.

Before production

Before launching a real service, add a privacy policy reviewed for your jurisdiction, disclose actual data processors, implement consent choices as required, secure forms, and provide a verified way to contact the operator.
