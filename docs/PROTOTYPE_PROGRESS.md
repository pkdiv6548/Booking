## Safety and product scope
Frontend-only demo. Train names, numbers, schedules, availability, fares and booking references are fictional. No live railway inventory, ticket issuance, payment, status feed, insurance, claims, refunds, invoice settlement or external messaging is connected. Do not enter real identity or payment data into the prototype.
