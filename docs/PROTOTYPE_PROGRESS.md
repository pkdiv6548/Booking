## Safety and product scope
Frontend-only demo. Airline names, flight numbers, schedules, fares, booking references and seat availability are fictional. No live airline inventory, ticket issuance, payment, status feed, insurance, claims, refunds, invoice settlement or external messaging is connected. Do not enter real identity or payment data into the prototype.
